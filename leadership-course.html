02 ~ why_leadership_fails

Why leadership fails

It's rarely a talent problem. It's almost always a systems problem — and systems can be fixed.

👻 handoff
"My last developer ghosted at 80%."

Work stalls at the finish line because there's no ownership interface — no clear definition of done, no accountability chain.

🔀 handoff
Handoffs between crews & PMs break.

Information dies in transit. Field leaders and project managers speak different languages because there's no shared protocol.

📉 scale
Leadership systems don't scale.

What worked with 3 people collapses at 30. The founder's habits become the bottleneck — and the company culture.
